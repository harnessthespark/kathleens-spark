🧠 Quick Wins for Busy Brains

When your brain needs extra support - no judgement, just gentle tools

🎯 One Thing Only

  1. Look at everything swirling in your head
  2. Pick just ONE thing - doesn't matter which
  3. Do only the very first step of that one thing
  4. Celebrate! That's enough. You did it, queen! 👑
💡 Your brain wants to hold everything at once. It can't. Choosing ONE is not giving up on the others - it's working WITH your fabulous brain.

⏰ Body Doubles

  1. Find a "body double" - someone to be present while you work
  2. Options: Video call a friend, put on a "study with me" video, work in a café
  3. You don't need them to help - just to BE there
  4. The presence of others helps your brain stay on track
💡 This isn't weakness - it's using your social brain to support your executive function. Very clever, actually!

💫 Energy-First Planning

  1. Check in: What's your energy RIGHT NOW? (High/Medium/Low)
  2. Match your task to your energy - not the other way around
  3. Low energy = simple, familiar tasks only
  4. Save complex stuff for your best energy windows
💡 Fighting your energy level rarely works. Working WITH it does.
